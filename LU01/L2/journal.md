## How a LLM works

A LLM just predicts what word comes next for any piece of text.
This assignes a possibility percentage for each word that could be next.
The modem will repeatedly run this through the algorithm every time.

AI will not always choose the highest posibility, and will choose at random.

The size of a LLM is the amount of training data and the amount of parameters.

Parameter:

- A knob that can tune a model. We do not actually know what each knob is doing.
  - Not an official defenition.

The model is initially completely random but is refined by large text.
Handfull to thousands of words. Give it all but the last word and see if it generates the last word corectly. Train it to pick the correct word with those "knobs".

**Similar tech:** Your phone keyboard predicts the next thing you may write.
