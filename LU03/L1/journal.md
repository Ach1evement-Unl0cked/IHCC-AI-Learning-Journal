# Journal

## MultiMedia

### Defenition

- Content that contains multiple forms of data
  - Video: Image/Audio
  - Anything that is not just text

### Terms

- Text-To-Speech TTS
  - Converting written text into natural-sounding speech

- Voice Cloning
  - Replicating specific voices from audio samples

- Text-To-Audio
  - Generating video content from text descriptions

- Image to Video
  - Animating static images into video sequences

### Prompt engineering for Better Results

#### Audio Generation

- Specify emotion and tone
  - Excited, Calm, Professional

- Include pacing intructions
  - Speak slowly, Emphesise the word "insert word here"

- Consider audiance
  - Child friendly voice, authorative narrator

#### Video Generation

- Use cinematic terms
  - Close-up, wide shot, tracking shot

- Specify Lighting
  - Golden Hour, Soft Lighting, Dramatic Voice

- Include style references
  - Documentary style, commercial look, artistic

### Audio Tips

- Use clear, well-punctuated text
- Break long sentences into shorter arguments
- Add pauses with commas and periods
- Test diffirent voices for your content type

### Video Tips

- Start with simple prompts and iterate
- Use high-quality source images
- Keep clips short (3-4 seconds) for best quality
  - 1 minute is about as long as you can go right now
- Combine multiple short clips rather than generating long videos
